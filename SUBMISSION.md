# Submission draft — update after live validation
**Project:** RecallDesk AI — Never Solve the Same Ticket Twice
**Track:** Challenge 02 — Agentic Support
**Pitch:** Support teams repeatedly diagnose issues because resolutions are trapped in individual sessions. RecallDesk coordinates triage, billing and technical support roles through one shared context layer, with verified learning and readable decision histories. The prototype contains 36 synthetic tickets, a reusable runbook, fixture-level approval, a baseline and a dashboard. The live Meko integration is designed to retrieve past context, save verified fixes, record conversations and publish evaluation artifacts. Local simulation is already runnable; real Meko results and model-backed decisions are pending.
